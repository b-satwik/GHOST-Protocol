DARKWATCH COMMAND QUICK REFERENCE: HOST vs. WHONIX WORKSTATION
========================================================================

HOST MACHINE (Docker Controlled Lab Environment):
Start Docker:
  commands/start_docker_lab.sh

Stop Docker:
  commands/stop_docker_lab.sh

Check Docker Status:
  commands/check_docker_lab.sh

------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHONIX WORKSTATION (Isolated Research Collector Environment):
Workstation Startup Info:
  ./commands/whonix/whonix_startup.sh

Check Tor Proxy:
  ./commands/whonix/whonix_check_tor.sh

Check DarkWatch .onion Service:
  ./commands/whonix/whonix_check_darkwatch.sh

Run Whonix Collector:
  ./commands/whonix/whonix_run_collector.sh

Open links:
  cat DARKWATCH_LAB_LINKS.txt
